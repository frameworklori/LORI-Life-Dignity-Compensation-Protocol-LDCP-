# LORI Life Dignity Compensation Protocol (LDCP)

LDCP is a dignity-first protocol for organizational response after fatal or life-altering incidents.

It is designed for companies, hospitals, governments, platforms, insurers, and public institutions that need a humane, transparent, and accountable process after severe harm occurs.

LDCP is not a replacement for law, courts, insurance, or professional counsel. It is an ethical governance protocol that can sit beside legal and institutional processes.

## Core Sequence

LDCP follows a four-stage response:

1. **Immediate Dignity Response**
   Provide urgent family support, funeral assistance, emergency funds, and humane contact before liability disputes dominate the process.

2. **Moral Accountability**
   Acknowledge harm, communicate clearly, and provide a direct apology or responsibility statement where appropriate.

3. **Safety Reform**
   Publish a prevention plan, correct unsafe systems, and document completed improvements.

4. **Family-Based Compensation**
   Evaluate compensation according to family impact, dependency, lost support, emotional harm, and long-term dignity rather than narrow cost-benefit logic.

LDCP also includes post-compensation follow-up and transparent safety-improvement disclosure.

## Practical Use Cases

LDCP can be adapted for:

- Workplace fatality or severe injury response
- Hospital or care-system harm response
- AI displacement or automation harm review
- Platform-related harm governance
- Public infrastructure accidents
- Insurance and compensation review boards
- Corporate social responsibility and trust-rebuilding processes

## Important Boundary

LDCP should not be used to:

- Avoid legal accountability
- Pressure families into settlement
- Replace court processes
- Hide safety failures
- Convert human life into a price
- Treat compensation as reputational damage control

The protocol is designed to protect dignity, transparency, and long-term accountability.

## Status
