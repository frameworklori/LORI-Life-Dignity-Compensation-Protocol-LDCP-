# LDCP Pilot Roadmap

## Phase 0: Preparation

Duration: 2 to 4 weeks.

Actions:

- Assign pilot owner.
- Select pilot domain.
- Review legal and privacy constraints.
- Choose 3 to 5 historical or synthetic cases.
- Confirm that no real family is contacted during dry-run testing.

Deliverables:

- Pilot charter
- Role assignments
- Case selection
- Privacy boundary

## Phase 1: Dry Run

Duration: 2 weeks.

Actions:

- Run each case through the LDCP sequence.
- Complete evidence worksheets.
- Generate review panel packets.
- Produce compensation recommendation drafts.
- Produce safety reform drafts.

Deliverables:

- Completed case packets
- Decision logs
- Compensation notes
- Safety reform notes
- Process friction list

Success criteria:

- Reviewers understand the process.
- Outputs are consistent enough to compare.
- No role confusion remains unresolved.
- Sensitive data handling is clear.

## Phase 2: Governance Review

Duration: 1 to 2 weeks.

Actions:

- Compare reviewer decisions.
- Identify scoring drift.
- Review appeal logic.
- Review anti-abuse controls.
- Check whether the process protects dignity without becoming formulaic.

Deliverables:

- Governance review memo
- Revised role guide
- Revised decision packet
- Pilot risk register

## Phase 3: Limited Live Pilot

Duration: 8 to 12 weeks.

Scope:

- Use non-fatal severe harm cases first where appropriate.
- Do not use LDCP to replace existing legal or insurance processes.
- Treat LDCP as an additional dignity and accountability layer.

Actions:

- Activate immediate response workflow.
- Assign family or affected-person liaison.
- Run accountability review.
- Draft safety reform plan.
- Conduct compensation recommendation review.
- Record follow-up commitments.

Deliverables:

- Live case packet
- Safety reform plan
- Follow-up schedule
- Transparency summary

## Phase 4: External Review

Duration: 2 to 4 weeks.

Actions:

- Invite independent ethics, legal, safety, or community reviewers.
- Review anonymized outputs.
- Identify bias or under-compensation risks.
- Confirm that families or affected people were not pressured.

Deliverables:

- External review memo
- Improvement backlog
- Deployment readiness decision

## Phase 5: Deployment Decision

Decision options:

- Continue as internal guideline
- Adopt for limited incident categories
- Expand to all severe harm cases
- Pause pending legal or governance changes
- Retire if harm risk outweighs benefit

## Pilot Metrics

Track:

- Response speed
- Family or affected-person comprehension
- Evidence completeness
- Review consistency
- Appeal rate
- Safety reform completion
- Follow-up completion
- Reviewer confidence
- Privacy incidents

## Stop Conditions

Pause the pilot if:

- Families report pressure or confusion.
- Legal counsel identifies rights-waiver risk.
- Sensitive data is mishandled.
- Review panels become symbolic.
- Compensation recommendations become purely cost-driven.
- Safety reform is not acted upon.

## Recommended First Pilot

Use a synthetic case set based on:

- Workplace severe injury
- Healthcare coverage denial
- AI displacement
- Platform harm
- Public infrastructure accident

This allows the protocol to be tested across domains before real deployment.
